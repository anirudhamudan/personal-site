Headroom

Claude usage limits in your Mac menu bar.

PlatformmacOS 13+BuiltOct 2026StackSwift
Download ↗GitHub ↗

Why I built it

I use Claude for most of my work, and the only way to see how much of my limit was left was to go and look for it. Usually I found out by running out, in the middle of something.

I wanted the number to just be there, the way battery percentage is.

What it does

  • Session usage at a glanceYour 5-hour usage sits in the menu bar, e.g. 48%.
  • Every limit, one clickHow much is used and when each one resets.
  • A quiet warningA small dot turns yellow when you're close, red when you're out.

Decisions I made

  • A dot, not a notificationGlanceable, never interrupting.
  • For people who don't live in TerminalSetup walks you through it instead of failing silently.
  • Risky things are opt-inAuto sign-in renewal is off until you turn it on.

DeskFM

A small lamp in your menu bar that starts your focus music in one click.

PlatformmacOS 14+BuiltOct 2026StackSwift
Download ↗GitHub ↗

Why I built it

I listen to the same lo-fi playlist every time I sit down to work. But if Spotify had been paused for a while, the Now Playing widget would disappear and the play key would open Apple Music instead. I'd end up searching for the playlist just to press play.

So I made a small lamp that lives in the menu bar and never goes away, with one button that starts my focus music.

What it does

  • Start Deep FocusOne click starts your go-to playlist, even if Spotify is closed.
  • Now playingTrack, artwork and a progress bar you can click to seek.
  • The basicsPlay/pause, skip, shuffle, launch at login.

Decisions I made

  • One job, done wellIt removes the steps between sitting down and hearing music.
  • Always visibleThe problem was a control that disappears, so the lamp never does.
  • No account, no sign-inIt talks to the Spotify app on your Mac directly.
